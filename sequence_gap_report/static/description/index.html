Sequence Gap Report

Why is invoice 00042 missing? Now you can answer.

Odoo sequences leave holes: a deleted draft, a discarded order, a cancelled entry, and the numbering jumps. Auditors and tax authorities ask about every one of them, and Odoo offers no way to find them. This module adds a read-only wizard that scans any numbered field of any model and lists every missing number, series by series, with the documents that sit just before and just after the hole. Free and open source (LGPL-3), Odoo 14.0 through 19.0.

  • Works on any model — invoices and journal entries (account.move), sales orders, purchase orders, transfers, or any other numbered document. Nothing to configure, no dependency on Accounting.
  • Understands any format — each number is split into its prefix and its trailing digits (INV/2026/00042), so every numbering series is analysed on its own and never mixed with another.
  • Shows the context — every gap comes with the document before and the document after it, so the hole can be explained on the spot.
  • Zero padding preserved — missing numbers are shown exactly as they would have been printed.
  • Fast and predictable — a single read of one column (up to 200,000 numbered documents per scan), gap detection in memory, a cap on the number of reported gaps, and a clear message when the report is truncated.
  • Honest about its limits — only documents you are allowed to read are counted, and the summary says so, because numbers hidden by access rights look exactly like gaps.
  • Strictly read-only — nothing is ever created, modified or renumbered, and your access rights still apply.

Installation

  1. Copy the sequence_gap_report folder into your addons directory.
  2. Open Apps, click Update Apps List, search for Sequence Gap Report.
  3. Click Install. Only the standard base module is required, so it installs on any database.

Configuration

  1. None. The report is available immediately under Settings → Sequence Gap Report.
  2. The wizard opens on account.move / name; type any other model and field if you scan something else.
  3. Maximum Gaps (default 500) caps how many gaps are listed; the report tells you when there are more.

Usage

  1. Go to Settings → Sequence Gap Report.
  2. Enter the model to scan (account.move, sale.order, purchase.order, stock.picking, …) and the field holding the number, usually name.
  3. Keep Include Archived ticked: archived documents still consumed their number, so they are not gaps.
  4. Click Scan for Gaps. Each line shows the first and last missing number of a hole, how many numbers it covers, and the documents immediately before and after it.
  5. Click Open Full List to sort, group or export the result; click New Scan to check another model.

Version notes

Identical behavior on every supported series (14.0 – 19.0).
